Cozy Arrows Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Cozy Arrows (package com.puzzlepawgames.cozyarrows) ("the app") uses. The app is made by Puzzle Paw Games ("we"), an independent developer.

Data the app keeps on your device

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress (solved levels and settings such as sound) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The app shows rewarded ads (for example, to earn a reward) and interstitial ads (full-screen ads between levels). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can give an approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps);
  • crash and performance data.

This data is handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Usage statistics

To understand how the game is played (for example which levels players finish or give up on), the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC. It may collect:

  • game events such as level started or finished, time taken and ads watched;
  • an app instance ID (a random ID for this installation, not your advertising ID);
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language, app version) and approximate location from your IP address.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, usage statistics are stored on your device and linked to the app instance ID only if you agree to storing and accessing information on your device in the consent form. Analytics in this app does not collect your advertising ID and is not used for ad personalisation. Google keeps this data under its own policies (see the links above); it is used only in aggregate to improve the game.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. Without consent you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app: open Settings and tap Privacy options.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security and sharing

We do not sell personal data. The app has no in-app purchases in its current version. Data sent by the AdMob and Firebase SDKs is encrypted in transit. We do not share data with anyone other than Google as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
