Paw Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Paw Puzzle ("the app") uses. The app is made by an independent developer ("we").

Data the app keeps on your device

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress (solved levels, unlocked puppies, settings such as sound) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can give an approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps);
  • crash and performance data.

This data is handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. Without consent you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy options button in the app's Settings.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security and sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data sent by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit. We do not share data with anyone other than Google as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
